My role

Design, review, iterate — Designed mobile login flows that are minimal and cover 14 account statuses across EA's mobile game portfolio.

Impact: In the first 2 weeks of launch, 47% of Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes players and 37% of Covet Fashion players logged into the EA App on PC Application.

Team: 2 PMs, 1 copywriter, ~10 developers

Timeline: 6 months

Tools: Figma, Zoom

Takeaways

Always work with different disciplines — I worked closely with devs, product, and marketing teams and gathered a lot of data to inform design decisions: what data we can collect from players entering their email, what boxes must remain unchecked in certain countries. Oversharing with the team smoothed the project.

Navigate through complicated organizations — In a large game company there are many layers of approval. Copies go through game studios, product, marketing, and legal teams for review. I componentized most screens for the fastest iteration possible, shared prototypes and grabbed feedback as early and often as possible.

Scope deliberately and measure early — We released the MVP to only 2 games first. As data started flowing in we grew more confident with the login flow and were ready to invest in further work, including drop-off point analysis to identify opportunities in the current release.

Start small, learn big — Scoping the release small meant we could act on real data rather than assumptions, and build confidence at each step before scaling to the full game portfolio.
