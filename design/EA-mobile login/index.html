Enabling players to login to their EA accounts on EA mobile games

My role

Design, review, iterate: I designed a Mobile login flows that are minimal, and cover 14 Account statuses

Impact: In the first 2 weeks of launching, 47% of Star Wars:Galaxy Of Heroes players and 37% of Covet Fashion players have logged into the EA App on PC Application.

TEAM: 2 PMs, 1 copy writer, and a team of 10 developers

TIMELINE: 6 months

TOOL: Figma, Zoom

Takeaways

Always work with different disciplines: I worked closely with devs, product, and marketing teams so I gathered a lot of data to inform my design: what data we can grab from players entering their email, what box we must leave uncheck in certain countries…oversharing to my team smoothed the project.

Navigate through complicated organizations: in a large game company there are many layers of approval. Copies go through the game studios, product team, marketing team, and finally legal teams for review. I componentized most of the screens for the fastest iteration possible, shared prototype and grabbed feedback as early/often as possible.

Incorporate the design into the whole service picture: In this design solution, I only focused on the design of the digital product, but considered less about the whole picture: how to initiate the feeding of initial users and meetups? How to encourage mentors to commit more time to helping others?

Start small, learn big: : we scoped the release small-only releasing the MVP to 2 games, and as data started flowing in we are more confident with the login flow, and was ready to spend more effort on further work including drop out point analysis to identify any opportunities in the current release.
